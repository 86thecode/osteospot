Speciality

Bruxism and jaw pain (TMJ) in Palma

At OsteoSpot I offer a specialised approach. Using structural, fascial, visceral, craniosacral and jaw-specific osteopathy, I work to release the joint.

Do you wake up with a headache or tooth pain? These symptoms are often caused by bruxism (clenching your teeth at night), a very common problem that directly affects the TMJ.

I use advanced stretching techniques on the inner and outer muscles of the mouth. This treatment relieves built-up tension, can reduce headaches and brings back comfort to your day.

What is osteopathy

The body works as a whole

A problem in one area can cause pain in another. Osteopathy looks for the source of the problem and treats it by hand, without machines or medication.

I combine structural, fascial, visceral, craniosacral and jaw osteopathy depending on what each person needs.

Your first visit

  2. 2I assess your mobility to find the source of the problem.
