FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between therapeutic massage and osteopathy?

Massage works mainly on the muscles. Osteopathy looks for the source of the problem in joints, fascia or organs. If you're not sure which to choose, message me and I'll advise you.

Is it a strong massage?

The pressure is adapted to you. It can be deep in tense areas, but I always respect your limits.
